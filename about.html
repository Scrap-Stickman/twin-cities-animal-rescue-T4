About Twin Cities Animal Rescue

Our Story

The rescue began when neighbors in Minneapolis started sharing foster space for dogs that had run out of time at municipal shelters. What started as a handful of living rooms is now a metro-wide network of fosters, drivers, and adoption partners.

We still keep the same promise: every animal in our care is treated as a future family member, not as inventory.

Pet Spotlight

Core Values

  • Honesty with adopters about behavior and medical needs.
  • Kindness toward animals and the people who care for them.
  • Action first: if a pet needs a ride, a crate, or a couch tonight, we find it.

Partner Acknowledgments

We thank metro veterinary clinics, municipal shelter partners, and local pet-supply donors who keep foster homes stocked. Their support lets community volunteers focus on matching pets with people.
