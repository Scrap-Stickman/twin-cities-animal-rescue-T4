Adoption, Foster, and Volunteer Services

Adoption Process Overview

Adopters start with an inquiry, meet the pet in a foster home or at a public event, and complete a home conversation with our adoption team. Approved adopters pay a modest fee that covers vaccines, a microchip, and a starter supply of food.

  1. Submit the inquiry form on our contact page.
  2. Meet the pet and talk through daily care needs.
  3. Sign the adoption agreement and schedule pickup.

Foster Program Overview

Foster homes give dogs and cats a quiet place to decompress before adoption. We provide food, medical care, and a coordinator you can text. Typical foster stays last two to six weeks.

Fostering is the fastest way we free kennel space for the next animal that needs us.
