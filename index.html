A Second Chance for Twin Cities Pets

Our Mission

Twin Cities Animal Rescue is a community nonprofit that finds safe homes for dogs and cats across Minneapolis and Saint Paul. We rescue animals from overcrowded shelters, provide foster care, and match each pet with an adopter who can meet its needs.

We want visitors to feel hopeful and ready to act, whether that means adopting, fostering, volunteering, or sharing our work with a neighbor.

Impact This Year

312 pets placed in adoptive homes.

86 active foster families across the metro.

140 community volunteers supporting transport, events, and daily care.
